Channels

The dial

Ordered by channel number. The bulletin board is a channel; a video channel plays a folder on the broadcast clock, with optional commercial breaks from a second folder; a guide channel lists what is on every channel on the dial; an external channel frames another page (a retro weather app).

The week as the schedule reads it: blocks are on-air windows.

Tuning

How the dial is turned: arrow keys and digits, a gamepad d-pad, or POST /api/tune from anything that can make a request. The remote control uses the HTTP bus.

Static and black also give a video channel a beat to load; a hard cut can flash blank.
The picture folds into one bright line, snaps to a dot, and the phosphor fades. Coming back, it blooms open.

Community Board

Identity

The name and tagline across the top of the board.

Page rotation

The board's lineup, in order, and how long each page holds. Each visit to a headlines slot shows the next item from that feed.

Community messages

Your bulletin board: birthdays, bake sales, lost dogs. Leave the color on Auto to let them cycle.

Facts & jokes

Two decks of one-liners, one per line. The rotation shows one at a time - Facts under a DID YOU KNOW card, dad jokes under a DAD JOKE card. Add either to the rotation above.

Weather

One weather strip, fed by Open-Meteo (free, no account). Search your town, pick the match, choose units. Add a Weather page to the rotation to put it on the air.

Auto-filled from the location; change only if needed.

Feeds

RSS or Atom sources for the headlines pages and the crawl. The id is how the rotation and crawl refer to a feed; the label is what viewers see. The server only ever fetches URLs listed here.

The crawl

The ticker along the bottom, and the fixed label pinned to its left.

Leave blank to hide the flag.

CheerLights

CheerLights is one global color anyone in the world can set. When this is on, the crawl carries the latest color as a ticker item, checked about once a minute. Write {color} where the color name should land.

Or: "Right now, the CheerLights color is {color}".

Music

Continuous background music behind the Community Board, played from the music/ folder - drop audio files there and they join the rotation. On a Pi kiosk, launch Chromium with --autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required so it plays from boot; on a desktop it starts on your first click.
