Timing

How fast pages turn, how often feeds refresh, and the daily reload.

CRT

For a real tube over composite or an RF modulator.

Composite and RF smear saturated red and blue and clip pure white. This calms both.
White text smears on some tubes. This only applies while CRT mode is on.

Feeds

RSS or Atom sources. The id is how the rotation and crawl refer to a feed; the label is what viewers see. The server only ever fetches URLs listed here.

Channels

The dial, ordered by channel number. The bulletin board is a channel; a video channel plays a folder on the broadcast clock; an external channel frames another page (a retro weather app, say). Tune with arrow keys, a gamepad d-pad, or POST /api/tune.

The week, as the schedule reads. Blocks are on-air windows.

Static and black also give a video channel a beat to load; a hard cut can flash blank.

Page rotation

Channel 82's lineup, in order. Each visit to a headlines slot shows the next item from that feed. Page seconds (above) applies here, not to video channels.

Community messages

Your bulletin board: birthdays, bake sales, lost dogs. Leave the color on Auto to let them cycle.

Facts & jokes

Two decks of one-liners, one per line. The rotation shows one at a time - Facts under a DID YOU KNOW card, dad jokes under a DAD JOKE card. Add either to the rotation above.

Weather

One weather strip, fed by Open-Meteo (free, no account). Search your town, pick the match, choose units. Add a Weather page to the rotation above to put it on the air. The AM/PM vs 24-hour choice follows the Clock format up in Identity.

Auto-filled from the location; change only if needed.

Music

Continuous background music behind the channel, played from the music/ folder - drop audio files there and they join the rotation. On a Pi kiosk, launch Chromium with --autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required so it plays from boot; on a desktop it starts on your first click.

CheerLights

CheerLights is one global color anyone in the world can set. When this is on, the crawl carries the latest color as a ticker item, checked about once a minute. Write {color} where the color name should land.

Or: "Right now, the CheerLights color is {color}".

The crawl

The ticker along the bottom, and the fixed label pinned to its left.

Leave blank to hide the flag.

Colors

Pages with no fixed color cycle through the palette you pick. The palette is broadcast-safe on purpose.
